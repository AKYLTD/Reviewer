# Photography brief — Roni's website

This document lists every photo slot on the website. Each row gives the
file the photographer should deliver, the aspect ratio, the page that
uses it, and a short shot description.

## Visual register (applies to every photo)

- **Light**: warm, soft, low-angle (early morning bake / late afternoon
  through windows). No hard flash, no clinical white-bouncing.
- **Surface**: warm wood (oak / walnut), aged ceramic, linen napkins,
  brushed brass. Avoid stainless steel, acrylic, mirrored.
- **Hands**: real hands of bakers/team members welcome. No models, no
  staged smiles.
- **Colour palette in-frame**: cream, brick (#C9483A), saffron
  (#F5A623), coffee (#4A1F08). Avoid bright greens, pure whites, blues.
- **Crop philosophy**: tight, considered, not editorial-vast. Closer is
  better than wider.

## Delivery

- 16-bit TIFF + colour-managed JPG at the listed resolution
- File names exactly as listed in the **Filename** column so the
  uploads-and-swap is mechanical (admin → Images → upload, then update
  the matching `<Photo src="/uploads/<filename>" />`).

## Total: 24 photographs

A half-day shoot covers the bagels-and-counter slots; the four shopfronts
need a separate run; the eight cake-builder shots are best done as a
single session with the kitchen baking the eight covers in sequence.

## Once the photographer delivers

1. Sign in to **/admin/images**, upload all 24 files.
2. Each photo lands at `/uploads/<filename>`.
3. Open each `<Photo mock="..." />` in the page source and add
   `src="/uploads/<filename>"`. The mockup steps aside; the real photo
   takes over.
4. Optionally pass `brandWash="none"` on the `<Photo>` to remove the
   warm tint overlay (the tint exists to make stock cohesive — once
   you have unique photography you can let it stand on its own).
